    How the score works

    1. Compare each signal with normal. Score = (latest − normal) ÷ typical swing, flipped so that positive always means better for households, capped at ±2.5.
    2. Measure the direction. Momentum = (latest − previous) ÷ typical swing, flipped the same way.
    3. Combine. Weighted averages give a level and a momentum for each lens. Leading signals weigh 1.5, the rest 1.
    4. Place it on the clock. Level sets left or right, momentum sets up or down. That gives four phases, the same idea as the ONS and Eurostat business cycle clocks.
    5. Pick the playbook. Phase plus social stability chooses the product feelings to design for.
    Index = 50 + 20 × weighted average score (50 is the UK's long-run normal; 30 is one typical swing worse)

    The normal and typical swing for each signal are my estimates from long-run UK data. Refine them once you download the full series.
