### 2. Flexible spending

Let people pause, shrink or spread costs instead of cancelling. Confidence: **Strong**. The need is holding steady.

**Why now**

- Direct Debit failure rate: 2.41% against a normal of 2.3% (worse than normal, getting worse)
- Consumer confidence (GfK): -22 against a normal of -11 (worse than normal, getting better)
- Bank Rate: 4% against a normal of 2.5% (worse than normal, getting worse)

**What to build**

- **Refill and smaller-pack versions of an existing product** (physical, 20% to 40% under the full size)
  - Test: Offer the refill to current customers first.
- **Pausable or skip-a-month subscription instead of a fixed one** (service, same price, more control)
  - Test: Add a pause button and measure cancellations saved.

**If the cycle turns:** Likely to fade if the cycle turns to recovery, so keep stock and fixed costs light.

**Would weaken if** these turn better for households: Direct Debit failure rate, Consumer confidence (GfK).

**Scorecard:** Logged call at 65%: Google searches in the UK for 'cancel subscription' and 'refill pouch' are higher in the 4 weeks to 27 February 2027 than in the same 4 weeks a year earlier.

- Personal finances, next 12 months (GfK): -5 against a normal of 0 (worse than normal, getting worse)
- Bank Rate: 4% against a normal of 2.5% (worse than normal, getting worse)

**What to build**

**If the cycle turns:** Likely to fade if the cycle turns to recovery, so keep stock and fixed costs light.

**Would weaken if** these turn better for households: Inflation expected in a year (Bank of England), Inflation now (CPI).

**Scorecard:** Logged call at 65%: Google searches in the UK for 'air fryer recipes' and 'draught excluder' are higher in the 4 weeks to 27 February 2027 than in the same 4 weeks a year earlier.
