# What Zeitgauge is for

**Read the mood of a place, then build what people will want next.**

People change what they buy before economists confirm a change in the economy. In a squeeze, they keep small luxuries and drop big ones. When trust falls, they buy safety and comfort. When a new technology is hyped, they buy tools to try it. Zeitgauge turns public data into a plain verdict on where a region stands, and then into a short list of product directions that suit the moment and the next phase.

## Principles

1. **Explain everything.** Every number on the dashboard can be traced to a source and a simple formula.
2. **Data drives the score. Theories are lenses.** Big ideas (Perez, Turchin, Strauss-Howe) sit beside the score and earn trust through scored forecasts.
3. **Design for the next phase too.** An idea that only works today is a bet. One that also works in the next phase is a business.
4. **Keep score.** Every month comes with forecasts, and accuracy is tracked.
5. **Phone first.** The dashboard has to work on a phone, in a minute.
